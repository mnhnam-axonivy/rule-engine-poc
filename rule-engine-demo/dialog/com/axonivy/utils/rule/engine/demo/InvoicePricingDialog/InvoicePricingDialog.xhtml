What decides what

One model, one call: the decisions build on each other (a decision requirements graph).
Part of dmn/invoice-pricing.dmnKindWhat it shows
lineDiscountTableDecision table, hit policy FIRSTRanges and lists in conditions; the first rule that matches wins. A function: called for every line.
vatRateTableDecision table, hit policy UNIQUEExactly one rule matches; two matching rules are an error.
taxFormulaFEEL literal expressionA formula that a developer can read, change and try above.
pricedLinesFEEL for line in lines return {…}Iterates over the lines and calls the three functions above.
orderDiscountsDecision table, hit policy COLLECTEvery rule that matches counts: discounts stack. A promotion valid between two dates.
orderDiscountPercentFEEL min(10, sum(…))The stacked discounts, capped.
shippingDecision table, hit policy FIRSTReads a sum over the priced lines as its input.
totalsFEEL contextSums of the priced lines plus shipping.
approvalFlags · approvalDecision table, COLLECT · FEEL contextEvery reason for an approval, and the highest approver they ask for.
